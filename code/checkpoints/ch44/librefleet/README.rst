LibreFleet manages a vehicle workshop's day to day: customers and their
vehicles, service orders that move through a kanban of stages, parts
consumption and margin tracking per order, technician assignment, and a
small OWL dashboard summarizing jobs per technician.

Booking conflicts are enforced at the model level: two service orders
cannot claim the same vehicle over an overlapping window. Orders with a
negative margin require manager approval through a wizard before they
can be confirmed.

Customers can follow their vehicle's service history from the portal,
and get a QWeb-generated PDF report once a job completes.
