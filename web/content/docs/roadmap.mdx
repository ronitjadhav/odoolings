**What changed in August 2026.** The tutorial grew from 40 to 50 chapters with two new
parts, 4 and 5, teaching the business apps Odoo actually ships: sales, purchase,
inventory, manufacturing, and the accounting core. They sit between building your own
module and extending Odoo's, because "extend the Sales flow" is not a thing you can
learn without knowing the Sales flow. They are taught the developer way: run the
business flow in the interface, then read exactly what it did to the database. The old
chapters 21 to 40 kept their content and moved to 31 to 50.

**What changed in September 2026.** Part 8 grew from four chapters to nine. Chapters 47
and 48 are the advanced OCA track: reading a real OCA repository at the scale an
integrator meets one, then learning from other people's merged pull requests. They sit
before chapter 52's migration work, which is what a reader does with both skills.

Then chapters 49 to 51 closed the gap those left behind, on a reader's request: six
chapters about OCA code and never once installing any of it. So now you put a real
module on the addons path and measure what it does, meet five modules that each hook
into Odoo by a different mechanism, and change one of them for your own project without
ever editing its code. Part 9's four chapters kept their content and moved twice, from
47 to 50, then up to 52 to 55.

**What changed in October 2026.** The tutorial grew a second dimension. The 55 chapters
are the journey, one pass per topic. Beside them, deep dives take one domain all the way
down, with their own chapter ids (`inv01`, so a track can grow without renumbering the
journey) and their own database. The Inventory deep dive is the first: fifteen chapters
and a boss challenge, written one at a time. The sidebar now shows the three tiers as
headings, every chapter carries a tier badge, and `odoolings start` can build the state
any chapter begins with, so you can read in any order.

All 55 chapters are now written, chapter 55 (Career Map) closing the tutorial. What's
left before launch is polish: M8's backfill of screenshots for the handful of early
chapters written before the screenshot policy existed, and a final read-through pass.
