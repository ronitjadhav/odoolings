**What changed in August 2026.** The tutorial grew from 40 to 50 chapters with two new
parts, 4 and 5, teaching the business apps Odoo actually ships: sales, purchase,
inventory, manufacturing, and the accounting core. They sit between building your own
module and extending Odoo's, because "extend the Sales flow" is not a thing you can
learn without knowing the Sales flow. They are taught the developer way: run the
business flow in the interface, then read exactly what it did to the database. The old
chapters 21 to 40 kept their content and moved to 31 to 50.
