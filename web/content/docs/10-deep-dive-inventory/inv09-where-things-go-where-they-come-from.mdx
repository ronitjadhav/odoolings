## Why this matters

So far every receipt ended in `LFD/Stock`, and every pick took "some" stock from there. A
real depot has shelves, racks and bays, and two questions come back all day. When the
truck arrives: *where does this go?* When an order comes in: *which of these do we take?*

Odoo answers the first with **putaway rules** and **storage categories**, the second with
**removal strategies**. Set them once, and the storekeeper's transfer already says "Shelf
B" and the picker's says "Fluids Rack". This chapter gives the depot real shelves, rules
that fill them by capacity, a bay that takes one pallet, and a sale where you watch the
removal strategy change which bottles leave.

## Concepts

### Putaway rules

A putaway rule says: *when this arrives in that location, store it in this sublocation*.
It has:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **When product arrives in** | the location the transfer delivers to, here `LFD/Stock` |
| **Product** / **Product Category** / **Package type** | what the rule is for; empty means everything |
| **Store to** | the sublocation to use instead |
| **Sublocation** | **No**: exactly Store to. **Last Used**: where this product went last time. **Closest Location**: the first child of Store to with the right storage category and room |
| **Having Category** | the storage category Closest Location looks for |
| **Priority** | the drag handle, to order rules by hand |

Odoo looks only at rules whose arrival location is the transfer's destination itself, not
its parents. Among the rules that match, the most specific wins: a package type rule,
then a product rule, then a rule on the product's own category, then on a parent category,
then a rule for everything. The result is written on each move line as its destination;
the transfer header still says `LFD/Stock`.

### Storage categories

A **storage category** describes what a location can hold. You give it to locations, and
Closest Location only uses locations that have it.

- **Max Weight**: the weight the location carries, counting the product weights.
- **Allow New Product**: **If the location is empty**, **If all products are same**, or
  **Allow mixed products**.
- **Capacity by Product**: at most this many units of one product.
- **Capacity by Package**: at most this many packages of one package type.

With Closest Location, Odoo walks the matching locations in name order. It first prefers
one that already holds the product (or the package type) and still has room, then any one
that can take it. If none can, the goods go to the rule's Store to location itself.

### Removal strategies

When a transfer reserves stock, the **removal strategy** decides which quants it takes
first:

| Strategy | Takes first | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **First In First Out (FIFO)** | the oldest arrival date | most goods; the default |
| **Last In First Out (LIFO)** | the newest arrival date | bulk goods where age does not matter |
| **Closest Location** | the location whose full name sorts first | picking along a fixed walking order |
| **Least Packages** | as few packages as possible, then the oldest | whole cartons rather than open ones |
| **First Expiry First Out (FEFO)** | the earliest removal date, then the oldest | goods with expiry dates (chapter 7) |

Odoo picks the strategy in this order: the product category's **Force Removal Strategy**,
else the location's **Removal Strategy**, else the nearest parent location that has one,
else FIFO. And whatever the strategy, quants with a lot come before quants without.

### Replenishments

A location's **Replenishments** box marks it as one the Replenishment report watches
(chapter 5): its own stock and its children's count as one. Each warehouse's stock location
has it; a parent and its child cannot both have it.

## Gotchas

- **Putaway never splits a line.** A Storage line of 25 pads fits no Parts Shelf (20
  each), so it lands in `LFD/Stock` itself. Split the line in **Details** if it should
  spread over shelves.
- **Rules belong to the exact arrival location.** A rule on `LFD` does nothing for a
  transfer into `LFD/Stock`. Put the rule where the transfer delivers.
- **"Closest" means first by name.** Both the Closest Location putaway and the removal
  strategy sort locations by their full name, not by distance in the building. Name
  shelves in walking order.
- **Max Weight 0 refuses anything with a weight.** It is not "no limit". As soon as the
  pads get a weight, Parts Shelf (Max Weight 0) accepts none of them, and putaway falls back
  to `LFD/Stock`. Set a real Max Weight on every category once products have weights.
- **A category strategy overrides the location for every product in it.** Had Brake
  Fluid been in Fluids, LIFO would have replaced the FEFO it needs. Group products by how
  they must leave, not only by what they are.
- **Made-to-order products do not reserve stock.** The pads have Replenish on Order
  (chapter 4), so a sale of pads waits for a manufacturing order and no removal strategy
  ever applies to them.

1. ⭐ Set the Fluids category's **Force Removal Strategy** to **Least Packages** and sell 12
   oil to Garage Nord. What does the Pick reserve, and why that and not the 13 loose
   bottles? Try **Closest Location** next (Unreserve, Check Availability), then cancel the
