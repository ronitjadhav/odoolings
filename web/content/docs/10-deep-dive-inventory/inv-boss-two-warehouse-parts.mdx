## The rules

This page is a *spec*, not a tutorial. No steps, no screenshots, nothing to copy. Work in
the `warehouse` database where chapter 15 left it, and **don't reopen the Inventory
chapters while you build**. Struggling for a while is the point: it is what turns
"followed along" into "can do". If you are truly blocked, read the *Concepts* section of
the chapter you need (never its Hands-on), close it, and carry on.

When you think you're done:

```console
$ python3 odoolings.py check inv-boss --db warehouse
```

Eight checks. All green means you can run a real multi-warehouse parts operation in Odoo
19. Only then open `seed_inv_boss` in `odoolings.py` and compare choices, not
correctness.

Made a mess of the database? The box at the top of this page rebuilds chapter 15's end
state in one command, and keeps a backup of what you had.

## The story

LibreFleet opens a small second depot in the north. It has no receiving dock worth the
name, so goods are put away the moment they arrive, but orders are still picked before
they are shipped. North never buys from vendors and never manufactures: everything it
holds comes from the main depot, LFD, and it reorders by itself when it runs low.

The first product it stocks is a new family of wear parts, starting with wiper blades.
Wear parts are valued first in, first out, and in North they always live on their own
shelf. The main depot already has 30 blades on its shelves from before the system went
live; they have to be brought in properly, at cost. Then North fills up, and its first
customer, Garage Sud, buys three.

## The spec

**Warehouse:**

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Warehouse | `LibreFleet North` |
| Short Name | `LFN` |
| Incoming Shipments | 1 step, straight into stock |
| Outgoing Shipments | 2 steps, pick then ship |
| Buy to Resupply, Manufacture to Resupply | both off |
| Resupply From | `LibreFleet Depot` |

**Storage:** an internal location `Shelf W` directly under `LFN/Stock`. A product
category `Wear Parts` under `Goods`, with Costing Method **First In First Out (FIFO)**.
One putaway rule: Wear Parts arriving in `LFN/Stock` are stored on `LFN/Stock/Shelf W`.

**Product:** `Wiper Blade`, a goods product that tracks inventory, in `Goods / Wear
Parts`.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Sales Price | $12 |
| Cost | $4 |

**Opening stock:** 30 blades counted into `LFD/Stock` through a physical inventory, with
the Inventory Reason `Opening stock`. The count must be worth $120, so the order of
your clicks matters.

**Replenishment:** one reordering rule for the blade, on `LFN/Stock`, min 5, max 20.
The rule itself names its route: the supply route North got from the main depot. A route
left empty on the rule, or set only on the product, does not meet the spec.

**Run it:** let the scheduler see the rule, then push the blades all the way through:
out of the main depot and into North, onto Shelf W, without ever choosing the shelf
yourself. Then sell 3 blades to Garage Sud from North (the sale's Warehouse decides
that) and deliver them.

**End state:**

| Where | Wiper Blades |
|---|---|
| `LFD/Stock` | 10 |
| `LFN/Stock/Shelf W` | 17 |
| anywhere else inside the company, transit included | 0 |

The 27 blades on hand are worth $108.

The names in this spec are fixed: odoolings looks up `LFN`, `LibreFleet North`, `Shelf
W`, `Goods / Wear Parts`, `Wiper Blade`, `Opening stock` and `Garage Sud`. Everything
else is your call.

## What the checks probe (so nothing feels unfair)

`odoolings check inv-boss` reads your database and changes nothing. It checks:

- the warehouse's name, its steps in and out, the two Resupply boxes and the warehouse it
  is resupplied from;
- the shelf, the category's costing method and the putaway rule, exactly one, from
  `LFN/Stock` to Shelf W;
- the blade's settings, then the done inventory moves with the reference `Opening stock`
  into `LFD/Stock`: their quantity and their value;
- the reordering rule's location, min, max and Route;
- that 20 blades went from the transit location straight onto Shelf W;
- that 3 blades reached Garage Sud through one of North's own transfers;
- where every blade is now, and what Odoo says they are worth.

One fair warning, since it is about the screen and not about Inventory: the **Route**
column of a reordering rule list is hidden until you turn it on from the column picker
at the right end of the header.

## Self-review, integrator style

After green, look at your work the way a colleague taking over the project would:

- Run the scheduler again. Does North order more blades? Say why from the rule's
  numbers before you look.
- Open any transfer of the resupply chain. Could someone who never saw you work find the
  rule that started it, from that transfer alone?
- Which of your settings would you have to change if North later bought blades straight
  from a vendor, and which would stay exactly as they are?
- If a second wear part arrives next month, how many things do you have to set up for it
  to land on Shelf W and be valued FIFO?
