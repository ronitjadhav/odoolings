**Goal:** know which batch or which unit went where, keep products that expire in date
order, and run a recall from one lot number. · **Time:** ~3 h · **Checkpoint:** none, this
chapter writes no module. It continues the `warehouse` database and installs Expiration
Dates.

## Why this matters

So far a bottle of oil was a bottle of oil: Odoo counted how many, never which one. For
many products that is not enough. A diagnostic tablet has a serial number on its back,
and the customer calls about *that* tablet. Brake fluid comes in batches that expire, and
when the supplier finds a bad batch, someone has to answer within the hour: where is it,
who got it, how much is left?

That is **traceability**, and in Odoo it is one switch on the product plus one record per
batch or unit. This chapter puts a serial number on every tablet, including the one
already on the shelf, receives brake fluid in two lots with expiry dates, lets Odoo pick
the lot that expires first, and finally recalls a lot from the customer and destroys it.

## Concepts

### By quantity, by lots, by serial number

Every storable product has a **Track Inventory** choice, once the **Lots & Serial
Numbers** setting is on:

| Tracking | What Odoo records | A quant says | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **By Quantity** | how many | 25 bottles of oil in LFD/Stock | anything interchangeable |
| **By Lots** | how many of each batch | 12 bottles of lot BF-002 in LFD/Stock | food, fluids, anything made or bought in batches |
| **By Unique Serial Number** | each single unit | tablet DT-0000002 in LFD/Stock | machines, tools, anything with a warranty |

A lot or serial number is a record of its own, a **lot** (`stock.lot`), with a name, the
product, an internal **Reference**, a description and its dates. Quants are split per
lot, so the shelf always says which lot is where. With tracking on, every move line must
name its lot before the transfer can be validated.

### Where lot numbers come from

Each operation type has two boxes, under **Lots/Serial Numbers**:

- **Create New**: this operation may invent new numbers. On by default for receipts and
  manufacturing.
- **Use Existing ones**: this operation picks among numbers already in stock. On by
  default for everything else.

On a receipt you can type the numbers, or open a line's **Details** and use:

- **Generate Serials/Lots**: give the first number and the count (or, for lots, the
  quantity per lot) and Odoo numbers the lines.
- **Import Serials/Lots**: paste a list, one number per line.

A product can have its own number series: its **Custom Lot/Serial** prefix (Inventory
tab) creates a sequence just for it, and the generator's **New** button takes the next
number from that sequence.

### Turning tracking on for a product you already have

Odoo lets you switch a product to lots or serials while it is in stock, and only warns:
the existing quants simply have no lot. To fix them, count them: in **Physical
Inventory**, set the line without a number to 0 and add a line with the number and the
real quantity. Applying the count moves the stock from "no lot" to its lot.

### Expiration dates

The **Expiration Dates** setting installs a module (`product_expiry`). A product gets four
day counts, and every lot received gets four dates from them:

| Date on the lot | Set by the product's | What happens when it passes |
|---|---|---|
| **Expiration Date** | days *after receipt* | the lot shows *Expired*; validating a transfer with it asks for confirmation |
| **Best Before Date** | days *before expiration* | nothing automatic; it is printed and shown for people |
| **Removal Date** | days *before expiration* | the lot leaves **Free to Use** and reservations skip it, but it still counts in **On Hand**; validating a transfer with it asks for confirmation |
| **Alert Date** | days *before expiration* | the lot shows *Expiring*, appears in the **Expiration Alerts** filter, and the daily scheduler logs an *Alert Date Reached* activity on it |

The dates are written on the lot at receipt. Changing the product's day counts later does
not touch lots already received. On the lot form, changing the Expiration Date moves the
three other dates by the same amount.

### First Expiry First Out

Which quant a reservation takes is the **removal strategy** of the location (or of the
product category). The default is **First In First Out**. With expiry dates, use **First
Expiry First Out (FEFO)**: the lot with the earliest removal date goes first, so nothing
sits on the shelf until it is too late.

### Following a lot

Every lot form has smart buttons to its **Sales**, its **Transfers** (deliveries), its
**Locations**, and the **Traceability** report: every done move of that lot, newest
first, from the vendor to the customer and back. A recall uses all of it:

<Mermaid
  label="A recall in four steps: find the lot's customers with the Traceability report, block the lot by moving its expiration date into the past, bring the delivered goods back with a return, and destroy everything with a scrap."
  chart={`flowchart LR
    FIND["Find<br/>Traceability report:<br/>who got the lot"] --> BLOCK["Block<br/>Expiration Date in the past:<br/>nobody can reserve it"]
    BLOCK --> BACK["Bring back<br/>Return on the delivery"]
    BACK --> DESTROY["Destroy<br/>Scrap the whole lot"]`}
/>

Three more settings live next to these: **Display Lots & Serial Numbers on Delivery
Slips**, **Display Lots & Serial Numbers on Invoices** (in the Valuation block) and
**Print GS1 Barcodes for Lots & Serial Numbers** (labels whose barcode carries the lot and
dates). The product's **Valuation by Lot/Serial** box gives each lot its own cost; the
valuation chapter comes back to it.

## Gotchas

- **Turning tracking on does not number the stock you have.** Odoo only warns. Until a
  count gives them numbers, the old quants have no lot, and any transfer that needs them
  will ask for one.
- **A serial typed by hand does not move the product's sequence.** The next **New** in the
  generator proposes the same number again. Either always use **New**, or type the next
  number yourself.
- **Expired stock is still On Hand.** Past its removal date a lot leaves Free to Use and
  reservations skip it, but stock counts, the Locations report and the On Hand column
  still show it. Scrap or return it; expiry does not make it disappear.
- **The expiry warning stops every transfer, not only deliveries.** Receipts, returns and
  internal moves of an expired lot all ask for confirmation, with a message about
  delivering.
- **Dates are written once, at receipt.** Changing the product's day counts later leaves
  existing lots alone. Fix a lot on its own form, where the four dates move together.
- **FEFO is a property of the location.** Set it on `LFD/Stock`, and products without
  dates in that location fall back to their arrival order.
