**Goal:** run two warehouses side by side, refill one from the other, and know where
stock is when it sits between them. · **Time:** ~2.5 h · **Checkpoint:** none, this chapter
writes no module. It continues the `warehouse` database and installs nothing new.

## Why this matters

LibreFleet's mechanics do not only work at the depot. A service van drives to broken-down
trucks with oil and brake pads on board. That van is a place where stock lives, so for
Odoo it is a **warehouse**: its own locations, its own receipts and deliveries, its own
numbers. And it needs refilling, from the depot, without anyone typing transfers.

Most real projects have more than one warehouse: a main store and shops, a factory and a
showroom, a depot and vans. The questions are always the same. How does stock move from
one to the other? Where is it while it travels? How much is in *each* one? This chapter
answers them on the depot you built, then adds two smaller tools that live on the same
screens: stock that belongs to a vendor (consignment) and instructions shown on a
customer's transfers.

## Concepts

### A warehouse is a bundle

Creating a warehouse creates much more than one record. For a warehouse with the Short
Name `VAN`, Odoo makes:

| What | Created | Active at first |
|---|---|---|
| a **view location** `VAN` and its children | Stock, Input, Output, Packing Zone, Quality Control, Pre-Production, Post-Production | only `VAN` and `VAN/Stock`; the others wake up with more steps |
| **operation types**, each with its own sequence | Receipts `VAN/IN/`, Delivery Orders `VAN/OUT/`, Internal Transfers `VAN/INT/`, Manufacturing `VAN/MO/`, and the step types (Pick, Pack, Storage, ...) | the four first |
| **routes** for its steps | *Service Van: Receive in 1 step*, *Service Van: Deliver in 1 step* | yes |
| **rules** on the global routes | a Buy rule, a Manufacture rule, MTO rules | as the Resupply boxes say |

So each warehouse numbers its own documents, and a picking always belongs to one
warehouse through its operation type. That is why the van's first receipt is
`VAN/IN/00001` while the depot is past `LFD/IN/00008`.

The second warehouse also switches on the **Multiple Warehouses** group by itself; there
is no setting to tick. From then on the warehouse form shows **Resupply From**, reports
get a **Warehouses** filter, and a sales order shows a **Warehouse** field (Other Info
tab). Archive the second warehouse and the group goes away again.

### How a warehouse gets its stock

The **Resupply** section of a warehouse form says where its stock comes from:

| Box | What it switches on |
|---|---|
| **Buy to Resupply** | the Buy route's rule for this warehouse: a rule can buy straight into it |
| **Manufacture to Resupply** | the Manufacture route's rule, and the Manufacturing operation type |
| **Resupply From** | one new route per warehouse ticked, named *Service Van: Supply Product from LibreFleet Depot* |

A new warehouse has Buy and Manufacture ticked. For a van that is wrong: the van should
never buy, it should be refilled. Untick both, tick the depot in Resupply From, and the
route that appears has three pull rules:

<Mermaid
  label="A need for oil in VAN/Stock pulls a van receipt from Inter-warehouse transit, which pulls a depot delivery from LFD/Output to transit, which pulls a depot Pick from LFD/Stock; the depot's own Pack step sits between Pick and Delivery."
  chart={`flowchart RL
    NEED["need: oil in VAN/Stock"] -- "pull" --> IN["VAN Receipts<br/>Inter-warehouse transit to VAN/Stock"]
    IN -- "pull" --> OUT["LFD Delivery Orders<br/>LFD/Output to Inter-warehouse transit"]
    OUT -- "pull" --> PICK["LFD Pick<br/>LFD/Stock onward"]
    PICK -. "push: the depot's Pack step" .-> OUT`}
/>

The supplying side is the depot's own delivery, so the depot's steps apply: a three-step
depot picks, packs and delivers even when the customer is its own van. The route is
selectable on products, product categories and warehouses, so you can put it on a
reordering rule, which is what this chapter does.

### Stock in transit

Between the depot's delivery and the van's receipt, the goods sit in **Inter-warehouse
transit**, a location of type *Transit* that belongs to the company. It existed from the
start, archived; the first resupply route switches it on.

Transit stock belongs to **no warehouse**. The depot's on-hand has already dropped, the
van's has not yet risen, and the product's total on-hand leaves it out. To see it, use
**Reporting → Locations** with the **Transit Locations** filter. This is the honest
picture of a truck on the road: the stock exists, nobody can sell it.

### How much is in each warehouse

A product's **On Hand** adds up every warehouse. To split it:

- **Reporting → Stock** has a **Warehouses** panel on the left: pick one and every column
  is for that warehouse only.
- A reordering rule's **Forecast** is for its own location, so the depot rule and the van
  rule for the same product show different numbers.
- In code, quantities take a context: `warehouse_id` for a whole warehouse, `location`
  for one location and its children.

A sales order ships from the warehouse in its **Warehouse** field. It defaults to the
salesperson's **Default Warehouse** (a field on the user), or to the first warehouse when
that is empty, so a sale from the van means changing it. A van driver's user would get
the van as Default Warehouse.

### Consignment: stock that is not yours

A vendor may leave goods on your shelf and be paid only for what you sell. Odoo models it
with an **owner** on the quant. The **Consignment** setting adds an **Assign Owner** field
to receipts; everything received that way keeps its owner through every later move.
Owned stock:

- counts in **On Hand** and can be reserved and delivered like your own;
- has **no value** in your stock valuation, whatever its cost;
- shows its owner in **Reporting → Locations**, in an **Owner** column.

Odoo does not bill you when you sell consigned goods; you settle with the vendor
yourself, from the deliveries.

### Partner-Specific Instructions

The **Partner-Specific Instructions** setting adds a text field, *Instructions on the
Stock Operations*, to the Notes tab of every contact. Any transfer for that contact, or
for one of its child addresses, shows the text in a blue banner. It warns, it never
blocks.

### More than one company, at a glance

Warehouses belong to one company, and so do their locations and quants (`company_id`).
A few locations are shared by every company: **Vendors** and **Customers** have no
company. Between two companies, goods would travel through **Inter-company transit**
instead, and turning a sale in one company into a purchase in the other is the
**Inter-Company Transactions** setting, which is Enterprise. This track stays in one
company.

## Gotchas

- **A new warehouse buys and manufactures for itself.** Buy to Resupply and Manufacture to
  Resupply are ticked by default, so a rule in the van could buy straight from a vendor.
  Untick them when a warehouse is meant to be refilled from another.
- **Resupply From appears only after a reload.** The field needs the Multiple Warehouses
  group, which the second warehouse switches on while the form is already drawn. The same
  goes for the Owner column after you tick Consignment.
- **Stock in transit belongs to no warehouse.** It has left the depot's on-hand and is not
  yet in the van's, and the product's total leaves it out. A stock count that forgets the
  Transit Locations filter "loses" every truck on the road.
- **The supplying warehouse's steps apply.** Refilling the van from a three-step depot
  means a Pick, a Pack and a delivery, for a box that only crosses the yard. For a quick
  move, an Internal Transfer from `LFD/Stock` to `VAN/Stock` skips transit and steps.
- **Consigned stock is sold like your own.** Deliveries reserve it without a warning, and
  Odoo does not bill you for it. Settle with the vendor from the deliveries, and keep in
  mind that your stock value does not include it.
- **The sales order's Warehouse decides who ships.** It defaults to the salesperson's
  Default Warehouse, else the first one, so a van sale left on the default ships from the
  depot.
